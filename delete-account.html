Delete Account

Last updated: May 1, 2026

You can delete your Dalnara account at any time. This page explains how to request account deletion and what happens to your data afterwards.

Delete from the App

The easiest way to delete your account is directly within the Dalnara app:

  1. Open Dalnara and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the Settings tab (bottom navigation bar).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Request via Email

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us:

support@dalnara.app

Please send the request from the email address associated with your Dalnara account. We will process your request within 7 business days.

What Gets Deleted

Upon account deletion, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your account credentials and profile information
  • All learning progress, lesson history, and SRS review records
  • Pronunciation scores and conversation transcripts
  • Vocabulary lists and saved content
  • Subscription and entitlement records
  • Any stored audio recordings

Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. All learning data will be deleted and cannot be recovered. If you have an active subscription, please cancel it separately through the App Store or Google Play before deleting your account.

Data Retention

We will delete your personal data within 30 days of your deletion request. Some anonymized, non-identifiable usage data may be retained for analytics purposes in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about account deletion or your data, please reach out:

  • Privacy inquiries: privacy@dalnara.app
